## Visual construction

Tweed-covered pine box with a rounded-corner rectangular grille opening framed by tweed on all four sides, which is what gives the TV-front name. Rectangular metal logo plate above the grille. Top-facing chrome control panel. Leather handle on top. Open back in two pieces.

**Specific to the selected year:** Fender says a light diagonal tweed pattern was used on TV-front amps from 1949. A very few TV-front Pros around 1952 were covered in white vinyl; do not use that for 1949. [fender-coverings](https://www.fender.com/articles/parts-and-accessories/tweed-tolex-two-tone-a-guide-to-amp-coverings)

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. The rounded-corner TV-front grille opening
2. Top-facing chrome panel with two knobs
3. One 15-inch speaker in a box only 8 in deep
4. Top-hung chassis with octal tubes hanging down
5. Metal logo plate above the grille

**Likely reusable parts:** tweed material from the 5E3 assembly (with a different weave direction); top-hung chassis approach from the 5E3; a new 15-inch speaker, which the Pano Verb and Vibrasonic also need.

**Still estimates:** Grille-frame radius and border widths. Handle. Everything inside the chassis.

## What's cool about it

- It is the big-speaker amp of the early lineup: one 15 in a box only 8 in deep. [rv-pro-1949](https://reverb.com/item/90479728-1949-fender-pro-amp-tv-front-re-tweed)
- The circuit is all octal tubes, with three 6SC7s up front, not the nine-pin 12AX7 family later Fenders used. [fender-pro-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Pro-5B5-Schematic.pdf)
- The 1949 specimen's dealer calls it "a big box Champ" with only Tone and Volume. [rv-pro-1949](https://reverb.com/item/90479728-1949-fender-pro-amp-tv-front-re-tweed)

## Uncertainty

- No Fender drawing marked 5A5 was found. The "PRO-AMP" sheet read here is filed by the retailer as 5B5 and shows no number on the part read. Its transformer number matches a dealer-described 1949 5A5, which suggests the circuit is close, but that is an inference.
- Field-coil versus permanent-magnet speaker for 1949 is not settled. One specimen has a 1949 P15N.
- Dimensions rest on one dealer listing of a re-covered amp.
- The Mojotone field guide page for this model was not found; only its newsgroup announcement turned up.
- Do not repeat the Buddy Holly association that appears on dealer pages without a better source.
