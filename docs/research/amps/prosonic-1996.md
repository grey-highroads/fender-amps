## Visual construction

A low, wide 2x10 combo in black, red or green covering with a silver grille and a tail logo. The panel has two channels with a three-way rectifier and class switch. The 1996 specimen below is in red with a cream panel and chicken-head-style knobs; confirm knob type at full size.

**Specific to the selected year:** One specification for the run per the manual. Colour is a factory option.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Low, wide 2x10 cabinet
2. Factory colour options: red, green or black
3. Rectifier and class switch on the panel
4. Two channels on one panel
5. GZ34 visible beside the power tubes

**Likely reusable parts:** 10-inch speakers from the Super Reverb study; tail logo and silver grille.

**Still estimates:** Panel artwork. Interior.

## What's cool about it

- One switch picks a tube or solid-state rectifier and Class A or AB, which changes the output from 30 to 60 W. [prosonic-service](https://music-electronics-forum.com/filedata/fetch?id=815674)
- It started in Fender's Custom Amp Shop; the combo schematic carries that name. [fender-prosonic-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Prosonic-Schematic.pdf)
- Fender offered it in red and green as well as black. [prosonic-manual-it](https://manualedistruzioni.it/fender/amplifier/prosonic/user-manual/28jt9)
