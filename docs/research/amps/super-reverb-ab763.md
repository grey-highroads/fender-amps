## Visual construction

Tall, nearly square black cabinet with four tens in a 2 x 2 grid, a Twin-style black panel at the top, tilt-back legs on the sides.

**Specific to the selected year:** Speaker brand and the logo style vary by year within 1963 to 1967. One dated late-1965 specimen has four CTS ceramic speakers and a raised tail logo on the grille. That is one amp, not a rule for the year.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Four tens in a square
2. Tall cabinet with tilt-back legs
3. Middle knob on one channel only
4. Large transformers
5. Speaker wiring harness joining four speakers

**Likely reusable parts:** deluxe-reverb-black and twin-reverb-early parts: black tolex, silver grille, skirted knobs, tail badge, strap handle, tilt-back legs; new 10-inch speaker, four used; new tall 4x10 cabinet.

**Still estimates:** Interior layout, baffle cutouts and speaker spacing. Logo and speaker brand now have one dated 1965 example.

## What's cool about it

- Four tens wired in parallel give a 2-ohm load, and the amp has a transformer built for exactly that. [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm) [mojo-4x10](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/2325/2729/files/Four-Speaker-Wiring.pdf)
- It has less negative feedback than the other two-6L6 blackface amps, as a side effect of that 2-ohm output. [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)
- It shares its "50-watt" power transformer with the blackface Bassman, a size up from the other 40-watt amps. [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)
- Only one channel gets a Middle knob, and the fixed mid voicing is fuller than a Deluxe Reverb's. [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

## Hotspot proposals

### 1. Four tens, two ohms

- **Anchor:** Cabinet front, the four speakers
- **Opening insight:** Four 8-ohm tens in parallel make a 2-ohm load, about as low as Fender went.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Pull two speakers or add a cabinet and the load changes a lot, which changes the feel and the stress on the amp.
- **Deeper notes:** Vintage amps used a "center tab" wiring pattern; later ones a plain parallel harness. Both give 2 ohms. Disconnecting two speakers gives 4 ohms.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. The Fender schematic draws four speakers in parallel across the output transformer secondary, plus an extension speaker jack. No impedance is printed. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [mojo-4x10](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/2325/2729/files/Four-Speaker-Wiring.pdf) [fg-sr](https://fenderguru.com/amp-models/super-reverb/) [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

### 2. A big transformer wound for 2 ohms

- **Anchor:** Chassis, output transformer
- **Opening insight:** The output transformer is large for a 40-watt amp and has a 2-ohm secondary.
- **Why it matters to a player:** It is credited with the tight, punchy low end that separates a Super from the smaller combos.
- **Deeper notes:** Part 125A9A, 4.2k primary to 2 ohms. The Vibrolux and Pro use 4k to 8 ohms.
- **Schematic check:** Part number confirmed as TR3, 125A9A. The 4.2k and 2-ohm figures are not on the drawing. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

### 3. Less feedback than its siblings

- **Anchor:** Chassis, negative feedback
- **Opening insight:** Because feedback is taken from a 2-ohm output, there is less of it than on other two-6L6 blackface amps.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Less feedback means a rawer edge and earlier breakup for an amp this size.
- **Deeper notes:** Same 820/100-ohm divider as the other multi-speaker amps. The source puts the reduction at about 41% of the feedback voltage.
- **Schematic check:** Divider confirmed: 820 ohms from the output to a 100-ohm resistor at the inverter. The 41% figure is analysis. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

### 4. One Middle knob

- **Anchor:** Front panel, Vibrato channel Middle knob
- **Opening insight:** Only the Vibrato channel has a Middle control.
- **Why it matters to a player:** It is the channel most people use, and the knob lets you fill in the scoop a Deluxe Reverb cannot.
- **Deeper notes:** The source also lists .022 µF mid capacitors, shared with the Concert, against .047 µF in other AB763 amps. Fenderguru describes the AA763 to AB763 change the other way round (0.033 to 0.022). Check the schematic.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed, and the conflict is settled for this circuit. The AB763 Super Reverb schematic shows .022 µF mid capacitors in both channels. The Normal channel has a fixed 6800-ohm resistor where the Vibrato channel has a 10k Middle pot. The AA763 drawing was not checked, so the direction of the earlier change is still unverified. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm) [fg-sr](https://fenderguru.com/amp-models/super-reverb/)

### 5. A Bassman-sized power transformer

- **Anchor:** Chassis, power transformer
- **Opening insight:** The power transformer is the "50-watt" type also used in the blackface Bassman.
- **Why it matters to a player:** More transformer than the other 40-watt amps is part of why the Super holds together when it is loud.
- **Deeper notes:** Part 125P5D, 360-0-360 VAC, giving about 460 V on the power tubes and 270 V on the preamp plates per the analysis.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. TR1 is 125P5D with 360 VAC each side. The drawing marks about 460 V on the 6L6GC plates, 465 V at the transformer centre tap and 270 V on the preamp plates, within 20%. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

### 6. Tube rectifier in a loud amp

- **Anchor:** Chassis, rectifier tube
- **Opening insight:** It keeps a GZ34 tube rectifier where the Twin and Showman went solid state.
- **Why it matters to a player:** A little give under attack in an amp that is nearly Twin-loud.
- **Deeper notes:** GZ34 on AA763 and AB763. The later AB568 used a 5U4GB.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. GZ34 on the AB763 drawing. The AB568 drawing was not checked. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm) [fg-sr](https://fenderguru.com/amp-models/super-reverb/)

### 7. Three stages on the reverb side

- **Anchor:** Front panel, Vibrato channel
- **Opening insight:** The Vibrato channel passes through three gain stages; the Normal channel does not.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The reverb channel has more gain and gets dirty sooner, and the two channels cannot simply be jumpered.
- **Deeper notes:** The channels are out of phase with each other, so jumpering needs a modification.
- **Schematic check:** Structure confirmed. The Vibrato channel has two 7025 stages, then a further half 7025 after the 3.3M and 10 pF reverb mix network. The Normal channel has two stages. The phase statement is analysis. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [rr-ab763-models](https://robrobinette.com/AB763_Model_Differences.htm)

### 8. Which tens are in there

- **Anchor:** Cabinet rear, speakers
- **Opening insight:** Early amps had Jensen C10R speakers; AB763-era amps came with CTS or Oxford 10L6.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Jensen, CTS and Oxford tens all turn up in these amps, in alnico and ceramic versions, so two original Super Reverbs can sound different for reasons that have nothing to do with the circuit. No source read here measures the difference; owners describe it.
- **Deeper notes:** Alnico and ceramic CTS versions both appear. Which one a given 1965 amp left with cannot be told from the model alone.
- **Schematic check:** Not a schematic fact. No speaker model is named on the drawing. One dated 1965 specimen has CTS ceramics. [fender-ab763-sr-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Super-Reverb-AB763-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fg-sr](https://fenderguru.com/amp-models/super-reverb/) [mojo-sr](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-blackface-super-reverb?page=3)

### 9. Pointing four tens at your head

- **Anchor:** Cabinet sides, tilt-back legs
- **Opening insight:** The legs are 19 inches long and tip the whole cabinet back.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Four tens beam straight ahead. Tilted, you hear what the audience hears.
- **Deeper notes:** Hardware length from the field guide. Leg geometry on the model is an estimate.
- **Schematic check:** Not a schematic fact.
- **Sources:** [mojo-sr](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-blackface-super-reverb?page=3)

## Uncertainty

- Second pass: the Fender AB763 Super Reverb schematic (drawing C-FD) was read. Each hotspot now carries a "Schematic check" line.
- Settled: mid capacitors are .022 µF in both channels on the AB763 drawing. The first pass recorded two secondary sources in conflict.
- Added: bright capacitor is 120 pF in each channel. The first pass had not captured it.
- Not on the drawing: output transformer impedances, the 41% feedback figure and the channel phase relationship. Keep these attributed to the analysis.
- The Fender layout for this model was not found, so board positions are still unverified. The 1965 specimen's chassis photos are the best interior evidence so far.
- Fenderguru starts production in 1964; the catalog, Mojotone and Ampwares say 1963.
- A photo of a "1965 Super Reverb schematic" is on Wikimedia Commons under CC0. The licence covers the uploader's photo. The drawing itself is Fender's, so do not treat it as cleared for display without advice.
