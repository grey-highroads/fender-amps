## Visual construction

Narrow-panel tweed cabinet, olive-stripe tweed, oxblood grille with gold stripe, cabinet-mounted script logo, flat brown leather handle, nail-in feet, black chicken-head knobs. Top chrome panel with five knobs, two more than a 5E3.

**Specific to the selected year:** The five-knob panel with Depth and Speed is what separates it from a Deluxe at a glance. Cabinet size is date dependent. Three June 1955 amps are small box. Later amps are large box. See Dimensions.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Five-knob top panel
2. Tremolo footswitch jack and cable
3. Cabinet size relative to the 5E3
4. Oxblood grille with gold stripe
5. Single 12-inch speaker

**Likely reusable parts:** deluxe-5e3 assembly (tweed, oxblood grille, chicken-head knobs, top chrome panel); speaker-jensen-p12r.

**Still estimates:** Panel spacing, cabinet size for the selected year, interior layout.

## What's cool about it

- This is the first Fender with an effect built in. Tremolo arrives here in 1955, eight years before reverb shows up in a combo. [mojo-tremolux](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-tremolux) [aw-tremolux](https://ampwares.com/category/amplifiers/fender/tremolux/)
- Under the tremolo it is close kin to the tweed Deluxe: same two 6V6s, same single twelve, same two-channel layout. [mojo-tremolux](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-tremolux) [mojo-5e3](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-deluxe)
- The name outlived the circuit. Later versions went fixed bias in tweed, then turned into a blonde piggyback head with 6L6s. [mojo-tremolux](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-tremolux) [aw-tremolux](https://ampwares.com/category/amplifiers/fender/tremolux/)

## Uncertainty

- Second pass: the cabinet question is answered for June 1955. Three first-batch amps are in the Deluxe-size small box. The size changed later; one seller says by late 1955.
- The same dealers describe the 5E9 generally as "a 5E3 in a taller cabinet". That is true of later amps, not the first batch. Do not carry it onto a 1955 model without a date.
- Output: 15 W or 18 W depending on source.
- Resolved 2026-11-20 (Q18): six tubes with a 5Y3GT, from the 0093 tube chart and the 5E9-A drawing. The original 5E9 drawing was not found.
- Tremolo circuit type (bias or signal) was not confirmed from a schematic. Do not describe the mechanism yet.
- Listings often label 1957 to 1960 amps "5E9" because the tube chart was not updated. Treat chart labels with care.
- Changed from first pass: "Cabinet size for 1955 rests on one dealer listing" is now three specimens from two dealers.
