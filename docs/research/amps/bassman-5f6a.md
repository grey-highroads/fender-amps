## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Four tens behind an oxblood grille
2. Top panel with six knobs and four inputs
3. GZ34 and two 5881s hanging from a top chassis
4. Two-piece open back
5. Four speaker jacks under the chassis

**Likely reusable parts:** tweed, grille, chicken-head knobs and top-hung chassis approach from the 5E3 assembly; the 10-inch speaker planned for the Super Reverb.

**Still estimates:** Interior positions beyond the order on the layout. There is no shared 5F6-A assembly in the repo yet; the explorer needs one first.

## What's cool about it

- Fender added a Middle control and four inputs on the 5F6, and the 5F6-A kept them. [fender-bassman-history](https://www.fender.com/articles/gear/going-low-the-history-of-the-bassman)
- It is the Bassman that changed to a long-tailed-pair inverter. [gw-bassman-history](https://guitarworld.com/features/fender-tweed-bassman-history) [ampbooks-bassman](https://www.ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/bassman-summary/)
- A secondary source says the 5F6-A schematic was copied directly by other makers. [wiki-bassman](https://en.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Fender_Bassman)
- A cathode follower drives the tone stack, so the tone controls sit after a low-impedance source. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)

## Hotspot proposals

### 1. Four inputs, one shared first tube

- **Anchor:** Top panel, the four input jacks
- **Opening insight:** Bright and Normal each get half of one 12AY7, and the two halves share a single cathode resistor.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The channels are not fully separate. What you do on one can affect the other.
- **Deeper notes:** Each input pair has two 68k resistors and a 1M to ground. Shared 820-ohm cathode resistor with a 250 µF 6 V bypass. 100k plate loads.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed on the Fender drawing. The secondary source agrees on the shared 820 ohms and 250 µF. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-5f6a](https://ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/fender-bassman-5F6-A/)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-5f6a](https://ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/fender-bassman-5F6-A/)

### 2. A bright cap on one channel only

- **Anchor:** Top panel, Volume Bright
- **Opening insight:** The Bright channel's volume pot has a small capacitor across it. The Normal channel's does not.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Below full volume the Bright channel lets more treble through.
- **Deeper notes:** .0001 µF (100 pF) on the schematic; the layout marks "100 MMF" on the Bright volume pot. Both volumes are 1M audio taper, mixed through 270k resistors.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-5f6a](https://ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/fender-bassman-5F6-A/)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-5f6a](https://ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/fender-bassman-5F6-A/)

### 3. A cathode follower before the tone stack

- **Anchor:** Chassis, second preamp tube
- **Opening insight:** The second 12AX7 is one gain stage straight into a cathode follower, and the follower feeds the tone controls.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The tone stack is driven hard from a low-impedance source, which is part of how this amp gets its breakup character. That consequence is a common analysis, not a Fender statement.
- **Deeper notes:** Gain stage with an 820-ohm cathode resistor and 100k plate load, direct-coupled to the follower with a 100k cathode load. The drawing marks about 180 V at the follower and 325 V on that supply node.
- **Schematic check:** Structure and values confirmed on the Fender drawing. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [rr-5f6a](https://robrobinette.com/5F6A_Modifications.htm)

### 4. Treble, Bass and Middle

- **Anchor:** Top panel, Treble, Bass and Middle
- **Opening insight:** This tone stack has a real Middle control, which most tweed amps did not.
- **Why it matters to a player:** You can fill in or scoop the mids instead of living with a fixed value.
- **Deeper notes:** Treble 250k linear, Bass 1M audio, Middle 25k linear. 250 pF treble capacitor, .02 µF bass and mid capacitors, 56k slope resistor.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed on the schematic and layout. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [fender-bassman-history](https://www.fender.com/articles/gear/going-low-the-history-of-the-bassman)

### 5. Presence in the feedback loop

- **Anchor:** Top panel, Presence
- **Opening insight:** Presence is not in the preamp. It sits in the negative feedback path from the speaker output.
- **Why it matters to a player:** It changes how much treble the feedback removes, so it acts on the power section's top end. This is standard engineering inference.
- **Deeper notes:** 27k feedback resistor from the output to the inverter; 5k linear Presence pot with a .1 µF 200 V capacitor.
- **Schematic check:** Values confirmed. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)

### 6. The long-tailed pair

- **Anchor:** Chassis, third 12AX7
- **Opening insight:** The phase inverter is a long-tailed pair, the design Fender carried into the 1960s.
- **Why it matters to a player:** It has gain of its own and contributes to the amp's drive when pushed. A secondary source traces the Bassman's change from paraphase to split-load to this design.
- **Deeper notes:** .02 µF input coupling, 1M grid resistors, 470-ohm bias and 10k tail resistors, 82k and 100k plate loads (5%), .1 µF 400 V coupling to 220k grid resistors on the 5881s.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-bassman](https://www.ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/bassman-summary/) [gw-bassman-history](https://guitarworld.com/features/fender-tweed-bassman-history)

### 7. Two 5881s and fixed bias

- **Anchor:** Chassis, the two power tubes
- **Opening insight:** Fixed bias, no adjustment, with 470-ohm 1 W screen resistors on each 5881.
- **Why it matters to a player:** More clean power than the cathode-biased tweeds, and the bias was not meant to be adjusted.
- **Deeper notes:** The drawing marks about 450 V on the plates and a negative bias near 42 V; read at zoom, treat the bias figure as approximate. The field guide says the bias rectifier was selenium.
- **Schematic check:** Screen resistors and the non-adjustable bias network confirmed; the bias voltage figure is hard to read on this scan. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [mojo-np-bassman](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-bassman)

### 8. GZ34 and a choke

- **Anchor:** Chassis, rectifier and choke
- **Opening insight:** A GZ34 rectifier feeds a choke-filtered supply.
- **Why it matters to a player:** A GZ34 sags less than the 5U4 rectifiers of the earlier tweed Bassmans. The comparison is with the field guide's 5D6 and 5E6 tube list.
- **Deeper notes:** 325 VAC each side, 20 µF 600 V filter sections, 4700-ohm and 10k dropping resistors, standby switch on the B+.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [mojo-np-bassman](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-bassman)

### 9. Four tens, four jacks

- **Anchor:** Cabinet rear, speakers
- **Opening insight:** Each speaker plugs into its own jack under the chassis, all wired in parallel for 2 ohms.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Pull a speaker and the load changes.
- **Deeper notes:** The schematic draws four speakers in parallel across the output.
- **Schematic check:** Parallel wiring confirmed. [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-5f6a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5F6A-Schematic.pdf) [mojo-np-bassman](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-narrow-panel-tweed-bassman)

## Uncertainty

- The runtime entry starts in 1958; the catalog groups 5F6 and 5F6-A from 1957. This mapping difference is already recorded in `timeline-map.json`.
- Bias voltage reads as about -42 V on a scan that is hard to read at that point.
- The CME specimen has a 5R4 rectifier fitted and replaced back panels. The factory GZ34 is from the drawing.
- Width varies by about an inch across sources.
- No shared 5F6-A assembly exists. Interior placement needs one before hotspots can be anchored.
