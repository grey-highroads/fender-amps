## Visual construction

A Twin Reverb control panel on top of a cabinet tall enough for three rows of two tens. Black tolex, blue/white/silver grille, chrome corners, casters, two strap handles on the sides, grille-mounted raised logo.

**Specific to the selected year:** Late-run amps added a line out, hum balance and a pull-boost switch. A 1972 example should have none of those.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Height: it should tower over a Twin
2. Six tens in a 2 x 3 grid
3. Twin-width chassis and panel at the top
4. Casters and side handles
5. Master volume knob on the panel

**Likely reusable parts:** twin-reverb-early chassis, silver panel artwork, skirted knobs, silver/aqua grille and black tolex; 10-inch speakers shared with the Super Reverb study; new tall cabinet, casters and side strap handles.

**Still estimates:** Speaker spacing, baffle layout, caster height, back panel arrangement.

## What's cool about it

- It is a master-volume Twin Reverb chassis in a different box. Fender's own advert said as much. [hendrix-supersix](https://hendrixguitars.com/Am070.htm) [wiki-sr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Super_Reverb)
- Six tens do not make a tidy impedance. The load comes out around 5.2 ohms and Fender simply used the Twin's 4-ohm transformer. [mojo-supersix](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-super-six-reverb)
- At 98 pounds on casters it is furniture. It exists because early-70s stages wanted more speaker area, not more gain. [hendrix-supersix](https://hendrixguitars.com/Am070.htm)

## Uncertainty

- Introduction year: 1970, 1971 or 1972.
- Load: 5.2 (published), 5.3 (forum quote) or 5.33 (calculated). The catalog uses 5.33.
- Dimensions come from one secondary source with no axis labels.
- The Freddie King association appears on a dealer page only. Do not repeat it without a better source.
- Second pass: the dealer page shows a Fender advert that calls the Super Six and Quad "new" and quotes a serial range for 1971. That supports an introduction before 1972 but is not a dated Fender document.
- One specimen has Jensen C10Q speakers per its seller. Stock speaker brand is not settled.
- The Fender spec sheet image on the dealer page was seen but not read at full size. It may carry factory dimensions.
