## Visual construction

Black tolex, silver-sparkle grille with raised logo, two twelves. Black front panel with the same control layout as the Vibrolux Reverb. Large chassis straps on top.

**Specific to the selected year:** 1965 amps sit right at the CBS change; front panel script may read either Fender Electric Instrument Co. or the later wording.

## Modeling priorities

1. 2x12 cabinet, 26.5 inches wide
2. Front panel shared with the Vibrolux Reverb
3. Rear panel with CBS header
4. Large chassis straps
5. Tilt-back legs

**Likely reusable parts:** whole front panel and chassis from the Vibrolux Reverb; black-panel parts from the Super Reverb and Princeton Reverb studies; 12-inch speaker from the Twin Reverb.

**Still estimates:** depth with hardware, strap geometry.

## What's cool about it

- Twin Reverb speakers with half the output tubes. [mojo_pr](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-blackface-pro-reverb) [pr_schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Pro-Reverb-AA165-Schematic.pdf)
- Higher plate voltage than the Vibrolux Reverb on the same two tubes. [pr_schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Pro-Reverb-AA165-Schematic.pdf) [vlr_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibrolux_Reverb_Amp_AA964.pdf?v=1758724723)
