## Visual construction

A low black head on a wider 2x12 cabinet. Black tolex, black, white and silver grille, raised chrome-and-black script logo on the grille, black strap handle with chrome caps, chrome two-hole corners, black skirted knobs, 16-inch tilt-back legs, thumb screws to hold the head. [mojo-bf-bassman](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-blackface-bassman)

**Specific to the selected year:** AB165 circuit from 1965. The August 1965 head below has a tailed logo on the grille.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Low black head on a wider cabinet
2. Two channels with Deep and Bright switches
3. 2x12 cabinet with tilt-back legs and thumb screws
4. Raised logo on the grille of both pieces
5. Solid-state rectifier, so no rectifier tube on the chassis

**Likely reusable parts:** black tolex, grille, skirted knobs, tail logo and tilt-back legs from the Twin and Deluxe Reverb studies; the piggyback head-and-cabinet form from the Showman study; the 12-inch speaker.

**Still estimates:** Cabinet height (21 or 20 in). Thumb-screw positions.

## What's cool about it

- The bass channel has a Deep switch where the normal channel has Bright. [mojo-bf-bassman](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-blackface-bassman) [fender-ab165-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-AB165-Schematic.pdf)
- The AB165 drawing tells you to reset a hum balance pot for minimum hum after new 6L6s. That is a balance control, not a bias adjuster. [fender-ab165-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-AB165-Schematic.pdf)
- It is the black-panel Bassman that carries the CBS-era "Columbia Records Distribution Corp." line on its drawing. [fender-ab165-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-AB165-Schematic.pdf)

## Builder check, November 21, 2026

Owner authorized the shared 1965-style AB165 head and horizontal 2x12 cabinet.
Both D-FE pages were rendered at 1900 px and read: six tubes (three 7025, one
12AT7, two 6L6GC), six rectifier diodes, six rotary controls, Deep in the Bass
channel tone network and 120 pF Bright at the Normal Volume. The drawing shows
a shared post-mix gain stage with 47 kΩ output feedback, and 220 kΩ output-plate
feedback paths. The balance network and minimum-hum service note are explicit.
These are circuit facts, not measured gain, distortion or stability results.

The 1965 head front photo was opened at 800 px for control order, knobs and
tailed badge. Its service work does not authenticate factory speaker fitment.
Use generic ceramic-format twelves at nominal 8 Ω each, in parallel for 4 Ω.
The cabinet remains closed; the internal-speaker topic is menu-only. No Showman
tone ring is copied onto this ordinary two-speaker baffle.

Head 22¼ W × 8 H × 9 D and cabinet 32 W × 21 H × 11½ D inches remain secondary
working envelopes. Q22's 20-inch reader correction remains unresolved. Tilt-back
leg length follows the secondary 16-inch reference; pivots and restraints are
estimates. No new measurement or universal stock speaker-maker claim.
