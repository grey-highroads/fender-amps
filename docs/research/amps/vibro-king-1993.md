## Visual construction

Wide combo with three tens, tilt-back legs, a panel split into reverb, tone and vibrato sections, ivory radio knobs and a red jewel. Early amps are commonly blonde with an oxblood or wheat grille; black with silver grille also exists.

**Specific to the selected year:** Covering and speaker brand vary across the run. Two 1993 specimens viewed in this pass are both blonde with a dark grille, a flat script logo, light-coloured knobs, chrome tilt-back legs and blue-frame speakers.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Three tens across the baffle
2. Three-section control panel with a dedicated reverb block
3. Ivory radio knobs
4. Blonde covering if 1993 evidence supports it
5. Tilt-back legs

**Likely reusable parts:** 10-inch speakers shared with the Super Reverb and Bassman studies; black tolex or new blonde tolex (shared with the Showman); new ivory radio knobs and three-section panel.

**Still estimates:** Speaker arrangement on the baffle, 1993 covering, dimensions for the first-year cabinet.

## What's cool about it

- It is not a reissue. It is a 1993 design that asks what a Fender could be if you combined the best bits of the old ones. [pg-vk](https://www.premierguitar.com/articles/Fender_Vibro_King_20th_Anniversary_Amp_Review)
- The reverb is the whole standalone 1963 reverb unit built in, with dwell, mix and tone, and it sits before the amp's gain. [zz-vk](https://www.zzounds.com/item--FEN8110000000)
- The first ones drove the reverb with an EL84. It ran hot, and Fender later swapped it for a 6V6. [fsup](https://support.fender.com/hc/en-gb/articles/42507659450779-What-tubes-are-in-my-amp) [pg-vk-sb](https://www.premierguitar.com/diy/silver-and-black/fender-vibro-king?rebelltitem=3)
- Three tens is a nod to the tweed Bandmaster, one of the least common Fender layouts. [zz-vk](https://www.zzounds.com/item--FEN8110000000)

## Uncertainty

- Height: Fender's manuals say 25.5 in. A retailer says 22.5 and a 1993 specimen about 23. Photos favour the lower figures for the cabinet body. Unresolved; see Dimensions.
- Corrected from first pass: the 25.5 in figure was attributed to a second retailer. It is Fender's own manual figure, for both the original and the Custom.
- Corrected from first pass: "No 1993 figure was found". A 1993 specimen figure and the original-amp manual figure are now recorded.
- Load: 2 ohms as labelled, 2.6 on one spec sheet, 2.67 by arithmetic. The original manual says 60 W into 2 ohms.
- Output tubes: 5881 in the owner's manual, 6L6 in Fender's tube table and in a dealer listing.
- One site reverses the EL84 and 6V6 order. Fender's own table and a 1993 specimen both support EL84 first.
- Rectifier for 1993 is still not confirmed.
- The reverb overheating story appears in classified ads. Do not repeat it as fact.
