## Visual construction

Black tolex, silver-sparkle grille cloth, chrome glides and corners, raised Fender logo on the grille. Black control panel facing forward with white script "Vibrolux Reverb-Amp" and "Fender Electric Instrument Co." on pre-CBS amps. Black skirted knobs numbered 1 to 10. Tilt-back legs.

**Specific to the selected year:** the drawing read is a CBS-era print. The first-year amps photographed are pre-CBS (charts "NL" December 1964 and "OB" February 1965) and carry the pre-CBS panel script.

## Modeling priorities

1. 2x10 black-panel cabinet, 25 inches wide
2. Front panel with 13 knobs and two Bright switches
3. Grille logo
4. Back panel with RCA reverb jacks and pedal jacks
5. Reverb tank bag in the cabinet bottom

**Likely reusable parts:** black-panel knobs, panel, tolex and sparkle grille from the Princeton Reverb and Super Reverb studies; 10-inch speaker from the Super Reverb study.

**Still estimates:** exact 1964 logo placement, cabinet depth with hardware.

## What's cool about it

- It packs the same reverb and optical tremolo circuits as the bigger black-panel combos into a two-ten cabinet. [vlr_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibrolux_Reverb_Amp_AA964.pdf?v=1758724723) [pr_schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Pro-Reverb-AA165-Schematic.pdf)
- No Middle knob; a fixed 6.8K resistor does the job. [vlr_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibrolux_Reverb_Amp_AA964.pdf?v=1758724723)
- The drawing has a bias-adjust pot. [vlr_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibrolux_Reverb_Amp_AA964.pdf?v=1758724723)
