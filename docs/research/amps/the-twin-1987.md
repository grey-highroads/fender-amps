## Visual construction

Twin-sized black combo with a black panel crowded with red knobs. Later amps in the run have black knobs. Limited coverings (snakeskin, white, red) were offered around 1989.

**Specific to the selected year:** Sources disagree on knob colour by year. The first pass recorded red until 1994. Mojotone lists red or black for 1990 to 1994. An original owner selling a 1988 white-snakeskin amp says its black knobs are original. Red is still the safest choice for a standard black 1988 amp, but it is not proven.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Red knobs
2. Two-channel panel with many more controls than a Twin Reverb
3. Deeper cabinet (12 in) than a vintage Twin
4. Rear-panel switchgear
5. Black tolex with silver grille

**Likely reusable parts:** black tolex and silver grille from the black-panel studies; two 12-inch speakers and Twin-width cabinet from twin-reverb-early; new red knobs and a dense two-row panel.

**Still estimates:** Panel layout and knob count, weight, logo style, handle type.

## What's cool about it

- It keeps the Twin name and the four 6L6s but adds a real gain channel. This is Fender answering the 1980s. [mojo-twin](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-red-knob-twin-the-twin) [fender-twinstory](https://www.fender.com/articles/behind-the-scenes/pristine-cleans-aggressive-overdrive-the-fender-twin-story)
- You can drop it from 100 watts to 25 with a switch, years before that was a common feature. [mojo-twin](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-red-knob-twin-the-twin)
- Bias and balance are adjustable from the back without pulling the chassis. [mojo-twin](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-red-knob-twin-the-twin)
- The stock speakers are wired in series for 16 ohms, the opposite of a vintage Twin's 4-ohm parallel pair. [mojo-twin](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-red-knob-twin-the-twin)

## Uncertainty

- Second pass: one page of the owner's manual and one dated 1988 specimen were read. Evidence is better but still thin. No standard black, red-knob specimen with a date was viewed.
- No Fender dimensions or weight were found. The 22 x 26 x 12 in figure is still from one field guide.
- Knob colour by year is in conflict. See Visual construction.
- The catalog's "4/8/16 selectable" load is supported by the owner's manual. Mojotone's 16 ohms describes the internal speakers in series.
- Fender's tube table lists The Twin as introduced in 1985; every other source says 1987.
- "Evil Twin" is used for this amp by players, but Fender applied that name to the 1994 Twin Amp. Avoid the nickname here.
- The full control order was not captured. The manual page read names the controls but not their sequence.
