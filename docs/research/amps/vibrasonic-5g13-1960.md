## Visual construction

A brown-panel combo with one 15. Forward-facing brown control panel, brown barrel knobs, flat grille-mounted logo, brown strap handle, chrome glides, large chassis straps. [mojo-vibrasonic](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-brownface-vibrasonic)

**Specific to the selected year:** Mojotone gives pinkish brown tolex with an oxblood grille for 1959 to 1961, and rough brown with a wheat grille later. The 1960 specimen below has the light brown covering and dark grille; the 1963 specimen has rough brown and wheat. ## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. One 15 in a wide brown combo
2. Forward-facing brown panel with nine knobs
3. Light brown covering with an oxblood grille for 1960
4. Large chassis straps and a full-width chassis
5. JBL D-130F with its aluminium dust dome

**Likely reusable parts:** brown panel, barrel knobs and covering shared with the Deluxe 6G3 and Showman studies; the new 15-inch speaker.

**Still estimates:** Covering colour. Interior positions.

## What's cool about it

- It was the first Fender with a diode rectifier and the first with a JBL as standard. [vg-vibrasonic](https://vintageguitar.com/27655/fender-6g13-a-vibrasonic)
- Its vibrato is the multi-tube harmonic type of the brown era, not the simpler bias tremolo. [mojo-vibrasonic](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-brownface-vibrasonic) [fender-5g13-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibrasonic-5G13-Schematic.pdf)
- Vintage Guitar notes it was by far Fender's most expensive amp per watt when new. [vg-vibrasonic](https://vintageguitar.com/27655/fender-6g13-a-vibrasonic)

## Uncertainty

- Output: 40 W in the catalog and in dealer listings; 50 W in Mojotone.
- "First tolex Fender" is often said of this amp. The source read here supports the rectifier and JBL firsts only. Do not state the tolex claim.
- The 6G13-A schematic exists at [fender-6g13a-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibrasonic-6G13A-Schematic.pdf) and was not read in this pass.
- Covering colour for a 1960 amp comes from one specimen photo and one field-guide line.
