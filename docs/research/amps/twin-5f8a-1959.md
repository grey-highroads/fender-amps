## Visual construction

Lacquered tweed, wide cabinet with the two twelves side by side behind brown-and-gold grille cloth. Chrome control panel on top, facing the back, reading from the player's left: Ground switch, Fuse 3 Amp, Off/On, Standby/On, jewel, Presence, Middle, Bass, Treble, Vol Bright, Vol Normal, two Bright inputs, two Normal inputs. Chicken-head knobs with 1 to 12 scales. The upper back panel has two oval cutouts (power tubes behind the left one); the lower back panel has a curved cutout.

**Specific to the selected year:** a 1959 5F8-A should have the side-by-side speaker baffle and the Middle knob. Both photo amps are dated March and April 1959.

## Modeling priorities

1. Wide tweed cabinet with two side-by-side twelves
2. Top control panel with eight knobs and four inputs
3. Upper back panel with two oval cutouts, lower panel with curved cutout
4. Four 5881s and the GZ34 visible through the back
5. Leather handle and tweed corner details

**Likely reusable parts:** tweed material, chicken-head knobs, jewel lamp and chrome panel from the 5E3 and 5F6-A studies; Jensen speaker from the tweed amps.

**Still estimates:** cabinet depth with hardware, back panel cutout geometry.

## What's cool about it

- The Middle knob arrives here, per Fender's own history. [fender_twin](https://www.fender.com/articles/behind-the-scenes/pristine-cleans-aggressive-overdrive-the-fender-twin-story)
- It has the long-tailed-pair inverter and standby switch that the big black-panel amps build on. [twin_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/fender_twin_5f8a.pdf?v=1758824597)
- Fender itself calls it 100 watts while everyone else says 80. It's worth a conversation either way. [fender_twin](https://www.fender.com/articles/behind-the-scenes/pristine-cleans-aggressive-overdrive-the-fender-twin-story)
