## Visual construction

Black tolex, black grille with white trim, raised chrome-and-black "Fender" script without a tail, black strap handle with chrome caps, black plastic corners, black skirted knobs numbered 1 to 10, chrome glides. A long black front panel with a dense row of knobs and pull switches. [mojo-75](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-75)

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Black silverface-era cabinet with a black panel and tailless logo
2. Long panel with pull-boost knobs and a lead section
3. One 15 (or 12) in a combo about 22 in wide
4. Dense rear panel with effects loop and line out
5. Solid-state bridge rectifier

**Likely reusable parts:** black tolex, skirted knobs and chrome glides from existing studies; the 15-inch speaker; the tailless logo is new.

**Still estimates:** Every dimension. Which format to model.

## What's cool about it

- It has a high/low power switch, from 75 W down to 15 W. [mojo-75](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-75) [12fret-75](https://archive.12fret.com/electronics/fender-75-12-inch-combo-amp-1980/)
- It has a separate lead drive and lead level, an early attempt at a Fender lead channel. [mojo-75](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-75) [rv-75-1980](https://reverb.com/item/86436931-fender-75-1980-black-face-model-75-75-watt-15-watt-switch-ed-jahns-designed-model-combo-amp)
- The drawing carries the Fender, Rogers and Rhodes logos, a CBS-era detail. [fender-75-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-75-Schematic.pdf)

## Uncertainty

- Format: the catalog and field guide list 1x12 and 1x15 combos and a head. The only specimen viewed is a 1x12. See Q20.
- Load: the catalog says 4, 8 or 16 selectable; the field guide says 8 ohms; the schematic has notes on reconnecting for 8-ohm outputs. The owner's manual was not found. See README conflicts.
- Designer credit is from a dealer and owner comments. No Fender document was read.
- The field guide's axis order is unclear for the combos.
