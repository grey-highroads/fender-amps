## Visual construction

Small black tolex cabinet, silver front panel with blue "Vibro Champ" script, skirted knobs, jewel lamp, toggle power switch. Grille cloth blue, white and silver. Open back with upper and lower panels. Rear label: "Fender Musical Instruments, CBS Musical Instruments Fullerton", Fuse 1A 125V, 0.5A 120V 60Hz, Speaker 3.2 Ohms 6 Watts RMS, Vibrato Pedal.

**Specific to the selected year:** no aluminum trim around the grille, which places it from 1970 on. The 1969 drip-edge amp in the photo set shows the earlier look for comparison only.

## Modeling priorities

1. Small cabinet, about 17 inches wide
2. Silver panel with five knobs, toggle and jewel
3. Blue-white-silver grille without trim
4. Open back with the 8-inch speaker visible
5. Rear label

**Likely reusable parts:** silver-panel knobs and panel material from the Super Six Reverb; cabinet proportions near the black-panel Champ.

**Still estimates:** speaker brand for a given year.

## What's cool about it

- The tremolo works on the driver stage, not the power tube. [vc_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibro-Champ_Amp_AA764.pdf?v=1758724309) [fenderguru_champ](https://fenderguru.com/amps/champ/)
- One 6V6 and an 8-inch speaker, so it breaks up at low volume. [vc_schem](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0604/9615/0624/files/Fender_Vibro-Champ_Amp_AA764.pdf?v=1758724309)

## Uncertainty

- The catalog row describes bias tremolo; the AA764 drawing does not (Q34).
- AA764 is a CBS Fullerton print. Whether the circuit changed during the 1968 to 1982 run was not checked.
- Speaker brand and impedance (3.2 vs 4 ohms label) varied.
