## Visual construction

Compact black combo with a top-mounted chrome panel and chicken-head knobs, like a small tweed layout in black tolex with silver grille. Particle-board cabinet on current versions.

**Specific to the selected year:** US-built green-board amps (1995 to 2001) versus Mexican cream-board amps (2001 on). Outwardly similar; the logo plate text differs.

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Small size next to a Hot Rod Deluxe
2. Top chrome panel with seven controls
3. Chicken-head knobs
4. Single twelve
5. Black tolex and silver grille

**Likely reusable parts:** black tolex and silver grille; chicken-head knobs and top chrome panel from deluxe-5e3; new 12-inch Eminence speaker.

**Still estimates:** Panel spacing, logo plate, rear panel.

## What's cool about it

- It runs EL84s, the tube most people associate with British amps, in a Fender-shaped box. [bj-service](https://music-electronics-forum.com/filedata/fetch?id=1000631) [wiki-bj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Blues_Junior)
- It has a master volume and a Fat switch, so it gets dirty at volumes a Deluxe Reverb cannot. [wiki-bj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Blues_Junior)
- The early green-board amps are known for a darker voice, and for reverb hiss because the reverb sits after the master volume. [wiki-bj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Blues_Junior)
- It may be the most-gigged small tube amp of the last thirty years, which is why it belongs next to the rarities. [wiki-bj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Blues_Junior)

## Uncertainty

- The "most popular" claim is common but unsourced. Phrase it as reputation, not fact.
- The service manual describes the speaker as a replica of a Jensen "P-10", which looks like a typo for a 12-inch model. Do not quote it.
- Tone differences between green and cream boards are player reports.
- Second pass: a December 1995 PR 295 specimen was viewed. The owner's manual pages photographed in that listing include a specifications page that was not read at full size.
- No photo of the green circuit board was viewed. Board colour is still from text sources.
