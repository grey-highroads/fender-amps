## Visual construction

TV-front cabinet like the Pro, with two panels: controls on top and a small fuse, switch and pilot panel low on the front or back. Closed back with two round ports. Chassis sits on the cabinet floor under the speaker.

**Specific to the selected year:** Mojotone says early 1952 amps could be covered in white vinyl instead of tweed. Fender's coverings article says white vinyl was used on a very few TV-front Bassman and Pro models around 1952. The one 1952 specimen viewed has covering painted black, so its finish is no guide. [mojo-bass-tv](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-tv-front-bassman) [fender-coverings](https://www.fender.com/articles/parts-and-accessories/tweed-tolex-two-tone-a-guide-to-amp-coverings)

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. TV-front grille frame, shared with the Pro
2. Closed back with two round ports
3. Chassis on the cabinet floor, not hung from the top
4. Separate top control panel and lower power panel
5. One 15-inch speaker

**Likely reusable parts:** the Pro TV-front cabinet once built; the new 15-inch speaker; chicken-head knobs from the 5E3.

**Still estimates:** Every dimension. Port size and spacing. Lower panel position.

## What's cool about it

- Fender built it for the Precision Bass. Before it was ready, the bass was paired with a TV-front Pro. [fender-bassman-history](https://www.fender.com/articles/gear/going-low-the-history-of-the-bassman)
- The chassis sits on the floor of the cabinet, with a cable running up to the control panel. Every later Fender combo hangs the chassis from the top. [mojo-bass-tv](https://mojotone.com/blogs/fender-field-guide/fender-tv-front-bassman) [fender-5b6-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5B6-Schematic.pdf)
- It has a closed back with two ports, which was unusual for Fender at the time. [pg-pbass-1952](https://www.premierguitar.com/articles/1952_Fender_Precision_Bass)
- It uses a paraphase inverter, the first of three inverter designs the Bassman would go through. [fender-5b6-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Bassman-5B6-Schematic.pdf) [ampbooks-bassman](https://www.ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/bassman-summary/)

## Uncertainty

- No dimensions for any 5B6. The Pro stand-in is an estimate.
- Production count: Wikipedia gives about 660 5B6 amps across TV-front and wide-panel cabinets; one dealer doubts its 1952 amp's serial run exceeded 20 to 25. Neither is a Fender figure. [wiki-bassman](https://en.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Fender_Bassman) [rv-bassman-1952](https://reverb.com/item/64607760-1952-tv-front-fender-bassman)
- Some dealers list TV-front Bassmans as 1951. The catalog and Fender's own history say 1952. [ccm-bassman-1951](https://www.creamcitymusic.com/g-e-smiths-vintage-1951-fender-bassman-26w-1x15-tube-combo-amp-tv-front-5b6/) [fender-bassman-history](https://www.fender.com/articles/gear/going-low-the-history-of-the-bassman)
- Wikipedia names 6SC7 or 6SL7GT preamp tubes and 5881 output tubes; Mojotone names 6L6GC. Tube types are not on the Fender drawing.
