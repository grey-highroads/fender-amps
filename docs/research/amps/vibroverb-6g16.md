## Visual construction

A brown-panel 2x10 combo: rough brown tolex, wheat grille, flat logo, brown barrel knobs on a forward-facing panel, tilt-back legs, raised handle. The 1963 specimen below shows all of this.

**Specific to the selected year:** Brown only. From 1964 the Vibroverb became a black-panel amp with one 15 and a different circuit. [wiki-vibroverb](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Vibroverb) [rf-vibroverb-1964-alt](https://retrofret.com/products/fender-vibroverb-6g16-tube-amplifier-1964-12138)

## Modeling priorities

Five features that most distinguish this amp:

1. Two tens in a brown combo, not the later single 15
2. Brown panel with nine knobs
3. Tilt-back legs
4. GZ34 and two 6L6GC on a full-width chassis
5. Reverb tank and its transformer

**Likely reusable parts:** brown panel, knobs and covering from the Deluxe 6G3 and Vibrasonic studies; 10-inch speakers from the Super Reverb study; tilt-back legs.

**Still estimates:** There is no shared 6G16 assembly in the repo yet. Reverb tank position.

## What's cool about it

- It is the first Fender combo with built-in reverb. [wiki-vibroverb](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Vibroverb) [pg-6g15](https://www.premierguitar.com/pro-advice/on-the-bench/fender-6g15-reverb-unit)
- Its tremolo moves the power-tube bias, unlike the optical tremolo of the black-panel amps that followed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf) [headstrong-6g16](https://headstrongamps.squarespace.com/blog/tag/srv)
- A builder describes its reverb send and recovery as used in no other Fender amp. That is a builder's claim. [headstrong-6g16](https://headstrongamps.squarespace.com/blog/tag/srv)

## Hotspot proposals

### 1. The first built-in reverb

- **Anchor:** Cabinet floor, reverb tank
- **Opening insight:** This is the amp where Fender put reverb inside the combo.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Before this, reverb came in a separate box.
- **Deeper notes:** Both halves of a 12AX7 drive the reverb transformer (TR4, 125A20A). Half a 7025 recovers the return, through a .003 µF capacitor into a 100k Reverb pot.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed on the Fender drawing. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [wiki-vibroverb](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Vibroverb) [pg-6g15](https://www.premierguitar.com/pro-advice/on-the-bench/fender-6g15-reverb-unit) [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)

### 2. Tremolo on the power tubes

- **Anchor:** Front panel, Speed and Intensity
- **Opening insight:** The tremolo swings the bias of the 6L6s, so it acts on both channels.
- **Why it matters to a player:** It throbs the whole amp, not just the Bright channel's preamp.
- **Deeper notes:** A 12AX7 oscillator with a 3M Speed pot; the 250k Intensity pot feeds the bias line. The drawing marks -52 V bias.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf) [wiki-vibroverb](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Vibroverb) [ampbooks-vibroverb](https://www.ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/vibroverb/)

### 3. A footswitch that grounds the oscillator

- **Anchor:** Rear panel, vibrato pedal jack
- **Opening insight:** The vibrato pedal stops the oscillator rather than switching the signal.
- **Why it matters to a player:** Tremolo off means the oscillator is shut down, not bypassed.
- **Deeper notes:** A secondary analysis says the footswitch shorts the oscillator's feedback path to ground.
- **Schematic check:** The pedal jack is drawn at the oscillator. The exact switching point was not traced on this scan. Not confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [ampbooks-vibroverb](https://www.ampbooks.com/mobile/classic-circuits/vibroverb/)

### 4. A bright cap on the Bright channel

- **Anchor:** Front panel, Bright channel Volume
- **Opening insight:** The Bright channel has a small capacitor across its volume pot.
- **Why it matters to a player:** More treble at lower volume settings on that channel.
- **Deeper notes:** 47 pF.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)

### 5. A tapped treble pot and a fixed middle

- **Anchor:** Front panel, Treble and Bass
- **Opening insight:** No Middle knob. The mid is fixed by a resistor, and the treble pot has a tap.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The mid voicing is set at the factory.
- **Deeper notes:** Treble and Bass 250k audio, 250 pF treble capacitor, 100k slope resistor, 6800-ohm fixed mid. The tap value reads as 70k on the layout and is unclear on the schematic scan; a builder also gives 70k.
- **Schematic check:** Stack values confirmed except the tap value. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf) [headstrong-6g16](https://headstrongamps.squarespace.com/blog/tag/srv)

### 6. The long-tailed pair

- **Anchor:** Chassis, phase inverter 7025
- **Opening insight:** A 7025 long-tailed pair feeds the 6L6GCs.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The same inverter family as the black-panel amps that followed.
- **Deeper notes:** 82k and 100k plate loads (5%), 820-ohm bias and 6800-ohm tail resistors, 1M grid resistors, .1 µF coupling to 220k grid resistors. 10k feedback resistor from the output.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)

### 7. GZ34 and two 6L6GC

- **Anchor:** Chassis, power section
- **Opening insight:** Tube rectifier, two 6L6GCs, 470-ohm 1 W screen resistors.
- **Why it matters to a player:** The GZ34 gives a little sag under hard playing.
- **Deeper notes:** 380 VAC each side; about 440 V on the plates; choke 125C1A; 16 µF 450 V filter sections; standby switch.
- **Schematic check:** Confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)

### 8. Two tens

- **Anchor:** Cabinet front, speakers
- **Opening insight:** The brown Vibroverb has two tens. The 1964 black-panel amp has one 15.
- **Why it matters to a player:** A different voice from the Vibroverb most players know.
- **Deeper notes:** Two speakers in parallel on the drawing.
- **Schematic check:** Parallel wiring confirmed. [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf)
- **Sources:** [fender-6g16-schem](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Vibroverb-6G16-Schematic.pdf) [rf-vibroverb-1964](https://retrofret.com/products/fender-vibroverb-6g16-tube-amplifier-1964-12138)

- The layout sheet in the same PDF looks like a later redraw. Use it for relative positions only.
- Treble pot tap value is not confirmed from the Fender schematic.
- Hotspot 3 is a secondary analysis, not confirmed on the drawing.
