# Princeton Reverb modeling packet

Prepared 2026-11-20 for the builder. Catalog row 67, stable ID `princeton-reverb-aa1164`. This packet answers the builder's request for one main specimen, the inspected views, and what is still an estimate. Dealer photographs are linked only. None are cleared for display on the site.

## Decision

Model **RetroFret item 9339, serial A-03894**, a 1965 black-panel Princeton Reverb. It is the only dated example found with front, rear, three-quarter, chassis top, chassis underside, transformer codes, pot codes and a dealer measurement in one gallery.

- **Dating evidence (dealer, checked against photos):** power transformer 125P1B coded 606-4-39 (week 39, 1964), which I read on the transformer in photo 8. Output transformer coded 606-5-07 per the dealer. Pots coded 304-6508 and 137-6506, which I read in photos 14 and 15 (both early 1965). Back panel reads "Fender Electric Instrument Co." The tube chart is worn; the dealer reads only the "O" year letter (1965).
- **What is not original (dealer statement):** speaker (now a Weber alnico 10-inch; I saw its "Classic Alnico" label in the rear photo), reverb tank and tubes "more recent", large filter can and some preamp electrolytics replaced (the fitted can is a modern CE Manufacturing part, visible in photo 13), cabinet patched at the lower right front side, one baffle mounting post replaced.
- **Second specimen for things A-03894 cannot show:** [Shore Point Vintage, Reverb 101149580](https://reverb.com/item/101149580-fender-princeton-reverb-1965-no-logo-black-panel), "very early 1965", no grille logo, original speaker, readable tube chart.

Source: [RetroFret 9339](https://retrofret.com/products/fender-princeton-reverb-tube-amplifier-1965-9339). Gallery images are `https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0672/8702/3756/files/NN_<id>.jpg` as listed in the table below. I viewed all 15 at full resolution (1333 x 2000 or 1600 x 1066) with cropping and, where needed, rotation.

## The rectifier: what the drawing says and what the amps carry

- The Fender AA1164 schematic (drawing K-FD) specifies a **5U4GB**. [Schematic](https://www.thetubestore.com/lib/thetubestore/schematics/Fender/Fender-Princeton-Reverb-AA1164-Schematic.pdf)
- The very early 1965 Shore Point amp's tube chart reads **"PRINCETON REVERB-AMP AA764"** and draws a **GZ34** in the rectifier position, then 6V6GT, 6V6GT, 12AX7, 12AX7, 12AT7, 7025 "left to right at rear", 117 V, 80 W. I read this from the listing's tube chart photo, rotated and enlarged.
- The fitted rectifier in A-03894 is GZ34-sized, but the tube is a later replacement per the dealer, so it does not tell us what left the factory.
- Forum builders report that AA764 charts with a GZ34 are common in amps built to the AA1164 circuit. Rob Robinette's suggestion was that Fender's paperwork did not track what was fitted; another member called the AA764 chart a clerical error and said an AA764 schematic in circulation is fake. Neither is documented. [TDPRI thread](https://www.tdpri.com/threads/aa1164-blackface-princeton-reverb-diylc-layout.916084/post-8857876). Another member notes the charts were printed in batches and kept in use after circuits changed. [TDPRI](https://www.tdpri.com/threads/fender-princeton-reverb-silverface-need-help-with-tube-selection-and-date-of-manufacture.1143791/post-12222288)
- No Fender AA764 drawing was found at the schematic retailer (checked by URL; it is not in their library).

**Status: unresolved.** What Fender shipped in an AA1164 Princeton Reverb is not established. The fitted bottle in A-03894 is a later replacement and says nothing about factory fitment. One amp's tube chart is evidence for that amp's paperwork, not for every amp. The disagreement between the drawing and the chart should be kept, not settled by the model.

**Recommendation:** model the rectifier socket and position, which all sources agree on. For the bottle, pick one shape as a placeholder and label it as such in the model notes; do not present it as the factory part. The hotspot should state the disagreement: the AA1164 drawing calls for a 5U4GB, and a dated early 1965 amp's chart calls for a GZ34. Revised 2026-11-20 after builder review; the first version of this packet recommended the GZ34 outright.

## Speaker

- A-03894's speaker is a replacement. The dealer says the original was an Oxford.
- The Shore Point amp's original speaker is an **Oxford 10J4** with code 465-506 and a blue Fender label on the magnet cover, read from its speaker photo. Oxford's code 465 and "506" read as 1965, week 6, which agrees with the dealer's "first few weeks of '65".

**Recommendation:** model an Oxford-style 10-inch with a blue magnet label. Basket and magnet dimensions are estimates.

## Grille logo

The earlier brief said a 1965 amp should have no logo. That was too strong. The Shore Point amp, with transformers and speaker from the first weeks of 1965, has no logo, and its dealer says it was built just before Fender began adding them. A-03894, with an output transformer from week 7 of 1965, has the raised logo, and RetroFret says the exterior is original.

**Recommendation:** follow the main specimen and fit the raised tail logo. If the builder wants the no-logo look, that is an early-1965 variant, not a different year. This is a wording change to the brief and a choice for the model.

## Other hardware

- **Handle:** black strap with nickel end caps on the top (photos 1, 3; the reissue spec also calls it a molded strap with nickel-plated caps).
- **Top strips:** two chrome strips run front to back on the top, one near each end (Shore Point top photo; also visible in A-03894 photo 1).
- **Corners:** none.
- **Bottom:** no photograph of the underside in either gallery. Glides or feet there are unconfirmed.

## Still estimates

1. Panel heights, knob spacing and the input and jewel offsets. They can be scaled from photo 1 against the 19.875 in width, but nothing was measured.
2. Back panel heights and the size of the gap between them.
3. Chassis length, depth and the spacing of tubes and transformers. The order is confirmed; the spacing is not.
4. Transformer and filter can sizes.
5. Reverb tank size and its position on the cabinet floor. A-03894's tank is a replacement and no photo shows it.
6. Speaker basket, magnet and baffle hole position.
7. Logo size and position on the grille.
8. Anything on the cabinet underside.
